# NV Newspage screenshots

Captured with [phpbb-release-tools](https://github.com/phpbbmodders/phpbb-release-tools)’ `phpbb-test-board/screenshots.py` on a disposable phpBB 3.3.19 board, with four example news topics. Unless a caption says otherwise, **Show the first image above the news** is on, and poster information and the post buttons are off; the other settings are the defaults.

## With poster information and post buttons

**Show user information** and **Show post-related buttons** on, seen by the admin, who gets every button.

<a href="newspage-news-full.png"><img src="newspage-news-full.png" width="640" alt="News page with poster information and post buttons"></a>

## Shortened news, no lead image

**Shorten news text** set to 120 characters and **Show the first image above the news** off. Longer news ends with "…", and **Read more** opens the whole post.
